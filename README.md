## Welcome to NeonKiez

Something strange is happening in the city.

Video-game worlds are beginning to break through into everyday places — and
NeonKiez is becoming a playground of arcade rules, pixel logic and impossible
shortcuts.

Grab your skateboard and explore.

---

## 🌆 The Kiez

NeonKiez is a freely explorable, Berlin-inspired urban world built around
streets, courtyards, canals, rooftops, shops, tunnels and hidden routes.

It is a place to learn rather than a collection of levels to select.

Landmarks matter. Shortcuts matter. And places that seem familiar may not stay
that way forever.

---

## 🕹️ The Games

Across the Kiez, ordinary places can suddenly play by very different rules.

Each game world takes inspiration from another era or genre of video-game
history — from stripped-down early arcade ideas to platforming, puzzles,
action and beyond.

They are not museum pieces.

They are part of the world.

---

## 🛹 Gameplay

- Explore a connected city from a top-down perspective
- Move through the Kiez on a skateboard
- Discover wildly different game worlds
- Learn new ways to move and interact
- Return to familiar places and find new possibilities
- Mix retro-game ideas inside one modern adventure

---

## ✨ The vibe

**NeonKiez** is bright, fast, playful and occasionally weird.

It draws from arcade history, pixel art, Berlin street life and the energy of
classic top-down action games — without the grimness that often comes with
them.

Made for younger players.

Not dumbed down for them.
